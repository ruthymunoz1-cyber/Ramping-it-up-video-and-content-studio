## Refund & Return Policy

Every Unity Keepsakes item is printed just for you when you order, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, wrong size or color chosen, or personalization text entered by the customer.

**We'll replace or refund your item if:**
- it arrives damaged or defective, or
- it isn't what you ordered (wrong design, wrong product, or a printing error on our side).

**How to request a replacement:** email **[support email]** within **30 days of delivery** with your order number and a photo of the problem. No need to send the item back.

**Personalized items:** we print your text **exactly as you type it**, including spelling, accents, capitalization and characters. Please check your preview before ordering. We can't offer refunds or replacements for spelling or typing errors made when ordering.

**Lost packages:** if tracking shows your order as delivered but you didn't receive it, contact us within **30 days** and we'll help.

**Cancellations:** orders go to production quickly. Contact us within **2 hours** of ordering if you need to cancel or change something. After that, we can't guarantee changes.

## Shipping Policy

**Where we ship:** worldwide, with a few exceptions where shipping isn't available. Our products are printed in production centers in the United States, Mexico, Canada, the United Kingdom and Europe, with partners in Japan, Australia and Brazil. Each order is printed at the center closest to you whenever possible.

**Processing time:** most orders are made in **2–5 business days** (personalized items and blankets may take a little longer).

**Delivery time after shipping (estimates):**
| Destination | Estimate |
|---|---|
| United States | 3–8 business days |
| Canada, UK, Europe | 4–10 business days |
| Rest of the world | 7–20 business days |

You'll get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

**International orders:** depending on your country, you may be charged **import duties, taxes or customs fees** on delivery. These are set by your country and aren't included in our prices.

We can't guarantee delivery dates, especially during the holidays, but we'll always do our best.
