## 1. Refund policy

Every Unity Keepsakes item is printed just for you when you order, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, a wrong size or color chosen, or personalization text entered by the customer.

**We'll replace or refund your item if:**
- it arrives damaged or defective, or
- it isn't what you ordered (wrong design, wrong product, or a printing error on our side).

**How to request a replacement:** email **hello@unitykeepsakes.com** within **30 days of delivery** with your order number and a photo of the problem. No need to send the item back.

**Personalized items:** we print your text exactly as you type it, including spelling, accents, capitalization and characters. Please check your preview before ordering. We can't offer refunds or replacements for spelling or typing errors made when ordering.

**Lost packages:** if tracking shows your order as delivered but you didn't receive it, contact us within 30 days and we'll help.

**Cancellations:** orders go to production quickly. Contact us within 2 hours of ordering if you need to cancel or change something. After that, we can't guarantee changes.

This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

## 2. Shipping policy

**Free shipping:** shipping is included in our prices on standard delivery.

**Processing time:** every item is made to order. Most orders are produced in 2–5 business days (blankets and personalized items may take a little longer).

**Delivery after shipping (estimate):** United States, 3–8 business days. You'll get a tracking number by email once your order ships. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

**🎄 Holiday orders:** to receive your order before December 25 in the US, please order by **Tuesday, December 8, 2026**. We can't guarantee delivery dates, especially during the holidays, but we'll always do our best.

**Where we ship:** we currently ship within the United States.

**Questions?** Email hello@unitykeepsakes.com.
