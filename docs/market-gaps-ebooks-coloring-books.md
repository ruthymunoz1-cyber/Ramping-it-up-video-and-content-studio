## 1. General ebook & coloring book gaps

### Coloring books — the standout: "Bold & Easy" large-print
Simple, thick-line designs at $8.99–$12.99, aimed at seniors, beginners, and low-vision
colorists. Named the #1 growth trend of 2025–2026 — demand is outpacing supply.
Large-print puzzles and coloring books for the 65+ population specifically are flagged
as underserved.

**Direct fit**: Coloring Book Studio already generates this exact style (bold outlines,
simplified detail) at any size — and it lands squarely on the existing MEMOIVA audience
(50+, cognitive wellness). Not a new niche to learn — existing tooling pointed at a gap
that already fits.

- [kdpeasy — coloring book trends 2026](https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/coloring-book-trends-2026)
- [kdpeasy — kids vs adult coloring books](https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/kids-vs-adult-coloring-books)

### Coloring books — other underserved sub-niches
- Axolotls specifically — "dominating" right now, only medium competition
- Combining two narrow themes at once: mandalas by anxiety symptom, historical fashion
  eras, hobby-specific sets (knitting, gardening tools)
- West African and Latin American folk patterns, indigenous-style geometric prints —
  flagged as still untapped for 2025–2026

### Ebooks — large-print/senior gap extends to crossword books
"Crossword puzzle books" is named an evergreen category; large-print puzzle books for
65+ are specifically flagged as underserved. The Crossword Studio's Puzzle Book compiler
is a direct fit for this.

Other currently-profitable named categories: career transition guides, niche health
topics (self-help/mental health for a specific audience, not general), micro-skill
development. Consistent pattern across all sources: narrow beats broad — "bold-line
mushroom coloring book for adults" outperforms "coloring book."

## 2. Melanin content — a real, sourced gap

### The market signal is real, not speculative
The coloring book category overall has grown 156% in two years (250K+ new monthly
searches, $1B+ annual market). Melanin-specific coloring books already sell on
marketplaces like Etsy ("Melanin Magic," "Melanin Gentlemen") — proven demand.

But what's currently selling there is largely PLR (private-label resold) content —
generic line art, recolored, not built with real skin-tone accuracy. **That is exactly
the gap this Studio does not have**: Character Lab is built around proper Monk Skin Tone
rendering with correct undertone and lighting at deeper tones, not "add brown" to
generic clip art.

### Children's books — the gap is structural, not a trend
Only about 30% of children's books published in the last two decades feature Black
characters at all — and even among books specifically meant to celebrate Black
children's experiences, representation still often falls short. The deeper issue: only
41% of 2024's published children's books had a BIPOC author, illustrator, or compiler
involved anywhere in the process.

**This is not a content gap to fill generically** — Book Outline's diverse picture book
template already exists for exactly this, with representation-forward guidance built
into the beats, not bolted on after.
