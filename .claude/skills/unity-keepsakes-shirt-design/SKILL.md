# Unity Keepsakes shirt design rules (locked)

## Canvas and safe zone
- Bella+Canvas 3001 front print: design canvas **1800 × 2400 px**, transparent background.
- **Start all text below y = 520 px** (the collar sits above that). Never place text in the top 22%.
- Keep text at least **300 px** from the bottom edge and **160 px** from the left and right edges.
- Vertically center the group inside the band from 520 px to 2100 px.

## Spacing (breathing room)
- Main headline to subline: **60 px** gap.
- Subline to small caps label: **30 px** gap.
- Never stack more than 3 text lines. Two is ideal.
- Leave at least **1× the headline's line height** of empty space around the whole group.

## Type sizes (on the 1800 px canvas)
- Headline: about **200 px** (Fredoka Bold). Break long headlines onto two lines with `<br>`.
- Subline (English or Spanish): about **88 px** italic serif (Playfair Display Italic).
- Small caps label: about **52 px**, letter-spaced 6 px (Montserrat SemiBold).

## Bilingual layout order
- Spanish headline first, English subline second, small caps label third.
- For English-first designs, put the English headline first and the Spanish subline second.
- Always check accents (¿ ¡ á é í ó ú ñ). Spell-check every Spanish line with the owner before it goes live.

## Colors (on white and natural tees)
- Navy `#1F2A44`, plum `#8C1C2C`, forest `#14532D`, gold-brown `#9A5B13`, navy-blue `#1F3A5F`.
- On dark (navy or black) tees, use the dark-version file with white and gold.

## Content rules
- No profanity, slurs, crude slang or innuendo. Keep it family-friendly.
- Faith items never share a design with humor or coffee (owner rule).
- Every design needs an English line or an English-first version.

## Output
- Save PNGs to `docs/unity-keepsakes/print-files/<batch>/` at 1800 × 2400.
- Preview five at a time in a contact sheet before linking to Printful.
