# Unity Keepsakes brand (locked)

Do not change these without the owner's approval.

## Logo
- Master file: `docs/unity-keepsakes/brand/logo-square-1200-v4.png`
- Structure, top to bottom: "Unity" (italic serif), "KEEPSAKES" (spaced sans), gold rule, "BILINGUAL KEEPSAKES" (pink), interlocked **U and K monogram** (gold U, teal K; K stem passes over the U bottom, K lower arm passes under), "VETERAN-OWNED" (teal).
- Gold ring with 12 colored dots around the edge.
- The veteran line must stay inside the ring.

## Colors
- Navy `#0E1E40`, plum `#701C54`, coral `#D6603C` (background gradient, navy to plum to coral)
- Gold `#F6CD6E`, cream `#FFF6E6`, teal `#40CDC8`, pink `#FF8CAA`

## Type
- Titles: Liberation Serif Italic / Bold (or Playfair Display on the web)
- Labels: sans-serif bold, letter-spaced

## Taglines
- Primary: "Bilingual Keepsakes for families who live, love and celebrate in two languages"
- Veteran: "VETERAN-OWNED" (English only). Owner is a veteran; do not claim certified status (SDVOSB etc.).
- Keep "Unity Keepsakes" as the name everywhere.

## Voice
- Warm, confident, elegant. Bilingual but never forced. No "certified" or government claims.
- Faith items stay separate from humor and coffee (owner rule).

## Store images
- Social share: 1200×630 (`social-share-1200x630-v3.png`, or its successor)
- Shopify: Settings → Brand, logo = square, social image = 1200×630.
